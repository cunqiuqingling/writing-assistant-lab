# External AI feedback copy / 外部AI反馈复制

Writing Assistant intentionally separates two AI workflows.

## Built-in AI reference analysis / 站内AI原文解析

The optional BYOK feature analyses only the reference sentence or paragraph. It does not read, send or evaluate learner writing, notes, plans or progress.

站内可选BYOK功能只解析当前参考原文，不读取、不发送、也不评价使用者的仿写、笔记、计划和进度。

## Copying learner work to an external AI / 将自己的写作复制到外部AI

After completing a sentence or paragraph exercise:

1. Click **复制本单元 · AI反馈**, **复制全部 · AI反馈**, **复制当前训练 · AI反馈** or **复制全部 · AI反馈**.
2. Open an AI platform you already use.
3. Paste the copied material and send it.

The copied text contains:

- the reference sentence or paragraph;
- the learner's own writing;
- optional notes or sentence-function labels;
- feedback instructions tailored to the current practice mode.

复制内容会自动包括：

- 参考句子或参考段落；
- 使用者自己的写作；
- 可选笔记或逐句功能标注；
- 与当前训练模式对应的反馈要求。

The generated instructions ask the external AI to identify problems and explain why before offering revision suggestions. They discourage immediate wholesale rewriting.

## Data boundary / 数据边界

Copying writes only to the device clipboard. Writing Assistant does not automatically send the copied material and does not call the configured BYOK provider.

Only after the visitor deliberately pastes and sends the text does it reach the selected external AI platform. That platform's privacy, retention, training and billing policies then apply.

复制操作只写入设备剪贴板，不会自动调用Writing Assistant中配置的API，也不会自动上传内容。只有使用者主动粘贴并发送时，自己的写作才会提交给所选外部AI平台；届时应同时查看该平台的数据与隐私规则。
